About SarkariJobs

SarkariJobs is an independent informational platform created to make government job, recruitment and examination-related information easier to discover, understand and access.

Who We Are

SarkariJobs is an independent online information platform focused on government jobs, recruitment notifications, examination updates and education-related opportunities.

Our goal is to organize important recruitment and examination information in a simple and easy-to-understand format so that visitors can quickly find the information they need.

We aim to provide clear information about vacancies, eligibility, important dates, application fees, selection processes, results, admit cards, answer keys and other relevant updates.

What We Provide

SarkariJobs covers multiple categories of government recruitment and examination-related information.

Latest Jobs

Government and other recruitment notifications, vacancy details and application information.

Results

Updates related to examination results, merit lists and result announcements.

Admit Cards

Information and links related to examination and recruitment admit cards.

Answer Keys

Answer key updates and examination-related information.

Admissions

Admission-related notifications and educational opportunities.

Syllabus

Examination syllabus and preparation-related information.

Our Purpose

Government recruitment information can often be spread across different official websites, notifications and examination portals.

SarkariJobs aims to make this information easier to discover by organizing important details in one place.

We focus on presenting information in a structured format so visitors can quickly understand the important details before visiting the relevant official website.

Information & Source Verification

We make reasonable efforts to research and organize recruitment and examination information using publicly available sources.

Wherever appropriate, we provide links to relevant official websites, recruitment portals and authorities so visitors can verify important information directly from the original source.

Important for applicants:

Recruitment dates, eligibility requirements, vacancy numbers, fees and other conditions can change. Applicants should always read the latest official notification before submitting an application or making a payment.

Independent Information Platform

SarkariJobs is an independent informational website. It is not a government department, government authority or official government portal.

Government departments, examination authorities, universities, boards and other organizations mentioned on our website remain independent entities.

Their names, logos, trademarks and official documents belong to their respective owners.

Our Approach

We aim to keep our website useful, simple and accessible for visitors searching for employment and examination information.

  • Organized recruitment information
  • Simple explanations
  • Clear important dates
  • Eligibility and vacancy details
  • Useful official links
  • Regular information updates where available
  • Mobile-friendly website experience

Accuracy and Updates

We make reasonable efforts to keep published information useful and updated.

However, official organizations may change recruitment schedules, eligibility conditions, fees, vacancies or other details after information has been published.

Therefore, visitors should verify important information through the relevant official notification or official website.

Feedback and Corrections

We value feedback from our visitors.

If you find an incorrect, outdated or incomplete piece of information on SarkariJobs, you can contact us with the relevant details.

We can then review the information and make appropriate corrections where necessary.
